Our Philosophy

Baked from a little kitchen with a lot of love.

"What started with a love for baking became a collection of cakes made for birthdays, celebrations and the little moments in between."

Maison Crumb was born out of a quiet devotion to real baking. In an era of hurried commercial kitchens and artificial shortcuts, we take our time. Every sponge is whipped by hand, every curd is slow-simmered, and every tier is textured with care.

We believe that a celebration cake isn't just dessert—it’s the joyful centrepiece of your memories, the moment everyone gathers around to smile, take photos, and make wishes.
